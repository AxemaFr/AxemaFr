## 👋 About me

I'm **Artem**, an engineering team lead at **[YCLIENTS](https://yclients.com/)** with 3 years of leadership experience.

- 🤖 **AI Team Lead** — I own the company's internal AI: frontend, platform and the AI harness our engineers build with
- 📱 **Previously Mobile Team Lead** — led the B2B mobile development team
- 🤝 Always happy to talk about AI in engineering, team building and mobile

## 🏆 Highlights

- 🚀 **Cordova → Flutter without stopping releases.** Migrated the B2B mobile app to Flutter while releases kept shipping as usual. [Read the story on the YCLIENTS tech blog →](https://tech.yclients.com/blog/cordova-to-flutter-migration/)

## 🧭 Leadership

What I do as a lead day to day:

| | |
| --- | --- |
| 🎯 **Ownership** | End-to-end responsibility for my area: roadmap, priorities, quality and delivery |
| 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 **Hiring** | Building the hiring funnel, interviewing, and onboarding new people |
| 📈 **Growth** | Individual development plans (IDPs), performance reviews, promotions |
| 💬 **1:1s** | Regular one-on-ones, feedback and keeping the team healthy |
| 💰 **Budgeting** | Planning headcount, tooling and infrastructure costs |
| ⚙️ **Process** | Engineering practices, release process and team rituals |
